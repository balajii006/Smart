The Tamil Nadu CCE grading system evaluates students continuously through formative and summative assessments. Formative assessments include activities, projects and class tests. Summative assessments are the term-end examinations.

Grades are awarded on a scale from A1 to E2 based on the total marks. A1 is given for marks above 90 percent and A2 for 81 to 90 percent.

The college library is open from 9 AM to 5 PM on working days. Students can borrow up to three books for 14 days.
